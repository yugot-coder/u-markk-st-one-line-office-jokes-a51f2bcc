One original one-line joke about office life for the One-Line Office Jokes bounty on make.fun.

> Our printer only jams on documents marked urgent, which I now believe is its actual job description.

## Why this line

- **Real office detail.** The printer jamming at the worst moment is the most universally
  lived office frustration, and "marked urgent" makes it concrete.
- **Punchline on the final beat.** The whole joke turns on the last three words —
  "its actual job description" — reframing the malfunction as the machine's purpose.
- **Clear on one reading.** No setup to explain, no wordplay that needs a second pass.
- **Fresh wording.** Not a recycled "this meeting could have been an email" line; the
  twist (the jam *is* the job) is its own.
- **Economy.** 100 characters, every word earning its place, nothing left to trim.

Ten candidates were drafted and compared in [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md); this line won
on the judge's own criteria.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line (100 characters, one line, no trailing newline) to
`<MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR>/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). Standard library only,
deterministic, no network. The program re-reads the file and asserts it is a single line
of 1–199 characters whose sha256 matches the line it intended to write.
