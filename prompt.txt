standard library only, deterministic) writes the chosen joke to
<MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR>/entry.txt (default /output/entry.txt). The gate requires
UTF-8 text of 1-199 characters, one line, not only whitespace. The judge is an
AI comedy editor comparing entries two at a time, weighing most: a single-line
office joke; a real office detail (meetings, email, printer, coffee machine);
the laugh landing in the last few words; clarity on one reading; fresh wording.
